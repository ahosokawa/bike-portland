PedalPDX

Bike-smart routing for Portland

Real bike infrastructure data from the City of Portland, color-coded on your map. Find the safest route — not just the shortest one.

Open PedalPDX

The difference

Every street, classified

PedalPDX takes the City of Portland's official bike network and lays it over every ridable street in the city, so routing knows the difference between a greenway and a four-lane arterial. Your route lights up so you always know what you're riding on.

Multi-Use Path Car-free paths like the Springwater Corridor and Eastbank Esplanade
Greenway / Buffered Lane Neighborhood greenways and lanes with physical buffers
Bike Lane Standard painted bike lanes and low-traffic streets
Caution Medium to high traffic — rideable but be alert
Avoid Difficult connections — used only when no alternative exists

Two modes

Pick your style

Bike Paths

Safest

Prioritizes multi-use paths, greenways, and buffered lanes, and crosses busy streets at signals. It will happily go well out of the way to put you on something like the Springwater.

Direct

Balanced

Shortest route that still favors low-traffic streets. For when you know the city and want to get there fast.

On the ride

Everything you need, nothing you don't

Turn-by-turn

Directions that name the street you're turning onto, spoken before each turn. Wander off course and it reroutes from where you are.

Custom routes

Build multi-waypoint routes through your favorite streets. Drag to adjust, save for later.

Works offline

The whole street network rides along on your phone, so routes are planned on the device itself — no server, no signal needed. Map tiles are cached too.

Get it

Install on your phone

PedalPDX is a web app — no app store needed. Add it to your home screen and it works just like a native app, including offline.

iPhone

1 Open PedalPDX in Safari
2 Tap the Share button (square with arrow)
3 Tap "Add to Home Screen"
4 Tap "Add"

Android

1 Open PedalPDX in Chrome
2 Tap the ⋮ menu (top right)
3 Tap "Install app" or "Add to Home Screen"
4 Tap "Install"
